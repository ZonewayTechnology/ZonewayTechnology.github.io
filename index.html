Shenzhen Zoneway Technology Co., Ltd.

Professional TFT LCD Touch Display & CTP Touch Panel Manufacturer | Integrated R&D, Production & Sales

Established in 2015, Shenzhen Zoneway Technology Co., Ltd. (深圳市中正威科技有限公司) is a national high-tech enterprise specializing in the R&D, manufacturing, and sales of TFT LCD displays, CTP capacitive touch panels, and fully laminated integrated modules (0.96 inch to 17.3 inch).

Company Overview & Manufacturing Capability

  • Facility Area: Over 4,000 m² modern manufacturing plant including a 2,000 m² dust-free clean workshop.
  • Production Lines: Equipped with 4 fully automated AOI inspection lines, 5 CTP touch panel lines, and 4 full-lamination lines.
  • Monthly Capacity: Up to 2,000,000 units.
  • Full In-House Process: Complete manufacturing integration including glass cutting, SMT, COG, FOG, CTP assembly, and TLCM optical bonding.
  • Quality Certifications: Certified with ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, and IATF 16949 (Automotive Quality Management System).

Product Applications

Our standardized and custom display assemblies are widely integrated across various industries:

  • Automotive & Yacht Displays (Rearview mirrors, central control, streaming displays)
  • Smart Home Appliances & Consumer Electronics
  • Industrial Control & Financial POS Terminals
  • Medical & Security Equipment, Face Recognition Systems, Computer Secondary Screens
