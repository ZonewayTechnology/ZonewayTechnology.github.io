Welcome to the official technical and engineering resource repository of **Shenzhen Zoneway Technology Co., Ltd.** (深圳市中正威科技有限公司). Established in 2015, Zoneway is a national high-tech enterprise specializing in the R&D, manufacturing, and sales of **TFT LCD display modules, CTP capacitive touch panels, and fully laminated (optical bonding) display assemblies**.

This repository serves as a centralized hub providing hardware datasheets, pinout definitions, drivers, 2D/3D CAD drawings, and integration guides for embedded engineers, system integrators, and B2B procurement teams.

---

## 🏭 Company Overview & Manufacturing Capability

* **Factory Scale:** 4,000+ m² modern facility in Longhua District, Shenzhen.
* **Monthly Capacity:** Up to 2,000,000 units.
* **Advanced Equipment:** 4 fully automated AOI inspection lines, 5 CTP touch panel lines, and 4 full-lamination lines.
* **In-House Processes:** Complete manufacturing integration including glass cutting, SMT, COG, FOG, CTP assembly, and TLCM optical bonding.
* **Quality Certifications:** **IATF 16949** (Automotive Quality Management System), **ISO 9001**, **ISO 14001**, and **ISO 45001**.
